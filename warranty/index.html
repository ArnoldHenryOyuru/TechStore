What is needed & exclusions

  • Original TechStore receipt or WhatsApp order confirmation, plus original box and cables where applicable.
  • For phones & tablets, Apple ID / Google account lock must be signed out prior to exchange or refund.
  • Physical drops, cracked screens, liquid exposure, power-surge burns, or third-party repair tampering are not covered under warranty.
  • Hygiene accessories (opened in-ear tips) are only returnable if confirmed defective.
